## [Atmosphere Detected on LHS 1140b, a Rocky Planet in Habitable Zone](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cy4kdd1e0ejo) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Astronomers using JWST report evidence of an atmosphere around LHS 1140b, a super-Earth in the habitable zone of a red dwarf 49 light-years away. It is described as the first atmosphere detected around a rocky, potentially habitable exoplanet. Confirming atmospheres on rocky planets around red dwarfs is a key step toward assessing habitability, since red dwarf systems are the most common in the galaxy and their intense stellar activity was thought to strip atmospheres away. LHS 1140b is about 5.6 Earth masses and 1.7 Earth radii, orbiting its star every 24.7 days. JWST emission spectroscopy reportedly rules out a mini-Neptune scenario, supporting a rocky composition possibly with a nitrogen-rich atmosphere or even a water/ice surface.

hackernews · neversaydie · Jul 17, 14:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48947560)

**Background**: Exoplanets in the 'habitable zone' orbit at distances where liquid water could exist on the surface. Red dwarfs are small, cool stars whose habitable zones lie very close in, exposing planets to intense flares and radiation. JWST uses transit and emission spectroscopy to analyze starlight filtering through or reflecting off exoplanet atmospheres, revealing their chemical composition.

## [Mozilla's First 'State of Open Source AI' Report](https://stateofopensource.ai/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Mozilla, in partnership with SlashData, released its inaugural 'State of Open Source AI' report, arguing that open models have nearly caught up to closed frontier models and are now used by 79% of developers adding AI functionality, versus 71% for closed models. The report signals a potential shift in AI power dynamics, challenging the business models of frontier labs like OpenAI and Anthropic while empowering hyperscalers, device makers, and independent developers who can run models without licensing fees. According to third-party data cited in discussion, open models' share of OpenRouter traffic rose from 40% to 63% in four months, with token volume growing nearly 5x; commentators peg open models as roughly 4-5 months behind the frontier but ~10x cheaper.

hackernews · rellem · Jul 17, 14:31 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48947825)

**Background**: 'Open source AI' typically refers to open-weight models (like Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek) whose weights can be downloaded and self-hosted, versus closed API-only models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. Mozilla, long an advocate for open web standards, is positioning itself as a leader of a 'rebel alliance' pushing for AI transparency and auditability.
